

-- motivation elements

motivations and reasons that guide the design and change of the EA
motivation elements work cross-functional hence can be used on each layer of EA
motivation elements do not form the separate layer of EA

stakeholder

individual, team or organization that has one or more interests in or concerns about organization and it's enterprise architecture
sets goals to achieve these interests and concerns
examples of stakeholders are CEO, directors board or customers

driver

condition that motivates and organization to define goals and implement changes
is called concern if being associated with stakeholder
examples of internal drivers are customer satisfaction or profitability
examples of external drivers are economic changes or new legislation

assessment

the result of an analisys of a specific driver
SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities and Threats) is a method of conducting a structured assessment
strenghts and weaknesses are internal, while opportunities and threats are external to the organization
the assessment findings should be addressed by adjusting existing goals or setting new ones
weaknesses and threats are problems tackled by negation goals
strenghts and opportunities translate into direct goals
examples of assessments are price competition pressure (threat) or high operational costs (weakness)

goal

high level direction for an organiztion and it's stakeholders
may be associated to concrete outcomes
examples of goals are increase profit or decrease operational costs

outcome

high level end result of some state of affairs
may the realization of defined goal
examples of outcomes are 5% profit increase yearly or reduce 100 helpdesk workers until end of the year

principle

statement that guides the design of solutons
example: system must be GDPR complient

requirement

properties of system elements that are needed to achieve goals
example: there must be a backup of data at the end of each day

constraint

limitations on aspects of the EA
in contrast to a requirement it does not define an intended functionality of the system

-- strategy layer

strategic direction and choices of the enterprise

express how the enterprise creates the value for it's stakeholders, the capability it provides, the resources needed to create the value and provides the capabilities as well as how the enterprise plans to configure and use these capabilities to achieve it's goals

resource
asset owned or controlled by an individual or an organizaion

capability
ability that an active structure element such as an organization, person or system posseses

value stream
sequence of activities that creates an overall results for customer, stakeholder or end user
